Experience

A Journey Through my IT Professional Career

Technical Support & Troubleshooting

 

As part of the operational team, I provide first- and second-line IT support to backdoor staff by resolving hardware, software, and network issues. This includes creating evaluation reports for damaged equipment that requires replacement.

Hardware & Peripheral Maintenance

 

To minimize operational delays, Our day-to-day task includes prioritizing on-site repairs over waiting months for corporate harware support for approvals. We routinely serviced essential branch hardware, including POS systems, workstations, printers, scanners, UPS, VoIP phones, server equipments, etc.

Sales Record Tracing & Data Processing

 

Acting as the technical bridge for the treasury and auditing departments to trace, reconcile, and troubleshoot electronic sales logs, POS data feeds, and revenue-sharing reports, we also Execute and monitor automated backup schedules which include in our SOD and EOD processes for critical databases, financial ledgers, and system archives to prevent data loss.

System & Network Maintenance

 

Monitoring and maintaining the reliability of the company's internet access is crucial since it is vital to the mall operations. Because the company's network infrastructure is entirely hosted locally rather than in the cloud hence uninterrupted connectivity is critical for Microsoft Outlook communications, executing secure FileZilla data transfers, and maintaining active server data transfer across branch and corporate operations.

 

Security & Surveillance Systems

 

In mall operations, security isn't just a department—it is the foundation of the guest experience. A retail environment can only thrive when safety is absolute and thieves aiming to steal from the mall are actively deterred. Consequently, maintaining the IT infrastructure behind the mall’s CCTV cameras and digital access control systems is crucial to safeguarding daily operations.

 

Password Management & Security Policies

 

It is also part of our task to enforce password policies, handling account lockouts, and facilitate secure onboarding and offboarding as staff members join or leave the organization.
